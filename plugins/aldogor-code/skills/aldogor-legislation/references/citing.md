# Citing legislation

A citation names the act in its canonical form and links to the version that was read. In a document's prose it is a brief inline reference with the link on it, as for literature.

## Italian state acts

- Form: `D.Lgs. 30 giugno 2003, n. 196, art. 2-septies`; `L. 23 dicembre 1978, n. 833`; `D.P.R.`, `D.P.C.M.` and `D.M.` likewise, a ministerial decree with its ministry. A decree-law is cited with its conversion law when the converted text is meant: `D.L. 19 maggio 2020, n. 34, convertito con modificazioni dalla L. 17 luglio 2020, n. 77`.
- Link: the Normattiva URN that `it-search` and `it-article` print, `https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.legislativo:2003-06-30;196~art2septies`, ending in `!vig=YYYY-MM-DD` when a past version is meant. An act type without a URN form, such as a ministerial decree, links to its Normattiva act page, which the helper prints.

## EU acts

- Form: `Regolamento (UE) 2016/679, art. 9`; for acts before 2015, `Direttiva 95/46/CE` and `Regolamento (CE) n. 1907/2006`.
- Link: the ELI for the act as published, `http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj`; the EUR-Lex page of the consolidated CELEX, `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/IT/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02016R0679-20160504`, when the consolidated text is meant.

## Piedmont

- Form: `L.R. 6 agosto 2007, n. 18, art. 3`; `Regolamento regionale 8 aprile 2024, n. 1`; the bulletin's acts as the BURP heads them, `D.G.R. 26 ottobre 2026, n. 13-3201` and `D.D. 23 ottobre 2026, n. 684`.
- Link: the Arianna page that `pie-law` prints, ending in `@YYYY-MM-DD` for a past version and `#artN` for an article; for a bulletin act, its BURP page, together with the act's type, date and number.
